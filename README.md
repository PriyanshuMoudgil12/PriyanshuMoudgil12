## 📌 My Projects

### 🏦 [Private vs PSU Banks in India — A Strategic Analysis (FY20–FY25)](https://github.com/PriyanshuMoudgil12/private-vs-psu-banks-india)

*SQL · Python · Tableau · Excel · Word*

Why private banks earn ~2× more per ₹ of assets than state-owned ones — and what the PSU CEO must change in the next 24 months. Six years of audited financials for India's six largest banks, ending in a 1-page board memo.
**Shows:** turning data into strategic recommendations a stakeholder can act on.

### 🛒 [Olist E-Commerce Sales Analysis](https://github.com/PriyanshuMoudgil12/olist-ecommerce-analysis)

*SQL · Python · Tableau · Excel*
