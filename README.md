## 📌 Projects

### 🏦 [Private vs PSU Banks in India — A Strategic Analysis (FY20–FY25)](https://github.com/PriyanshuMoudgil12/private-vs-psu-banks-india)

*SQL · Python · Tableau · Excel · Word · statsmodels*

Six years of audited financials for India's six largest banks (FY20–FY25). The **~1pp Private-vs-PSU ROA gap is highly statistically significant** — Welch's *t* = 5.54, *p* ≈ 5 × 10⁻⁶, 95% CI on the gap = **[0.64 pp, 1.34 pp]**. An **OLS regression of ROA on NIM, NPA, CASA explains 80% of the variance** (R² = 0.80), and after those structural controls the private-vs-PSU dummy is **not significant (*p* = 0.37)**. The gap is a NIM story (84% of it) — not an ownership story. Ends in a 1-page board memo with 3 strategic recommendations for the PSU CEO.

**Shows:** financial-statement reading, hypothesis testing, OLS regression interpretation, panel data work, executive communication.

### 🛒 [Olist E-Commerce Sales Analysis](https://github.com/PriyanshuMoudgil12/olist-ecommerce-analysis)

*SQL · Python · Tableau · Excel · SciPy*

99,441 orders across 9 relational tables in a SQLite warehouse. Two headline findings:

- **State-level Pearson *r* = −0.86** between late-delivery rate and review score — the strongest single relationship in the data. The Northeast is structurally underserved.
- **Cohort analysis surfaces a 1.17% 90-day repeat rate** across 93K customers. Olist isn't a marketplace with a retention problem — it's a *near-zero-retention* marketplace. That reframes the geographic concentration in finding #2 from "current weakness" to "structural CAC risk."

Translated into 5 prioritized business recommendations in the executive summary.

**Shows:** production-grade SQL across 9 joined tables, cohort analysis (using `customer_unique_id`, not the Olist per-order `customer_id` trap), correlation analysis, recommendation framing.
